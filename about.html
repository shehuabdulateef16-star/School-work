THE IDEA

Small tools.
One student hub.

Project Fluid is growing from a simple collection of calculators into a broader student command center.

01

Useful first

Calculations, study sessions, quizzes, planning, notes and revision tools should be quick to access and easy to understand.

02

Built to grow

The V2 foundation is still lightweight and browser-based. That makes it easy to improve the experience now while leaving room for accounts, cloud sync and more advanced features later.

03

Made for momentum

The goal is simple: remove little bits of friction from student life and help you keep moving.

OUR APPROACH

Useful first.

02
